Privacy policy

Lockdown poster · updated 2 October 2026

Who uses it

Lockdown poster is used only by the owner of the Lockdown YouTube channel, on that channel. It has no other users.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent through Google's OAuth screen, it uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the owner's channel (youtube.upload) and to read the status of the channel's own videos (youtube.readonly).

What it stores

One OAuth refresh token for the owner's channel, kept on the owner's own server, and the video files and texts the owner prepared for upload. It collects no personal data from viewers or from anyone else.

Sharing

Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising or analytics. Lockdown poster's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the stored token then stops working and is deleted.

Contact

The support email shown on Google's consent screen for Lockdown poster.
